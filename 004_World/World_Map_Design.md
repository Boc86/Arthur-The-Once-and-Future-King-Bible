# World Vision

Britain is a fractured land recovering from conflict.

The player explores:

- Kingdoms
- Forests
- Ruins
- Castles
- Mythical locations

---

# Major Regions

## Camelot

The future centre of Arthurian civilisation.

## The Wild Lands

Ancient forests filled with forgotten magic.

## Mordreds Territory

A corrupted land controlled by enemies of Camelot.
