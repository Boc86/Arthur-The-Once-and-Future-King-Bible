# Arthur: The Once and Future King
# Game Vision

Version 0.1

---

# 1. Vision Statement

Arthur: The Once and Future King is a dark fantasy action RPG where players rebuild a broken legend.

The player takes the role of Arthur Pendragon, resurrected by Guinevere into a world where history itself has fractured.

Across countless possible versions of Britain, Arthur must recover the lost Knights of the Round Table, restore Camelot, reclaim legendary relics, and prove himself worthy of the throne.

The game combines:

- The combat depth of modern action RPGs.
- The replayability of roguelites.
- The build experimentation of loot-driven games.
- The narrative depth of Arthurian mythology.

---

# 2. The Player Promise

When a player starts the game, they should immediately understand:

"I am rebuilding Camelot."

Not:

"I am clearing another dungeon."

The player's journey is not simply becoming stronger.

The player's journey is restoring something that was lost.

---

# 3. Target Player Experience

The game should create five primary emotions.

---

# 3.1 Power

The player should feel like a legendary warrior.

Combat should communicate:

- Impact
- Weight
- Skill
- Control

Attacks should feel meaningful.

Weapons should feel different.

Abilities should create memorable moments.

---

# 3.2 Discovery

The player should constantly wonder:

"What will I find this run?"

Examples:

- A lost Knight
- A legendary weapon
- A forgotten village
- A corrupted version of Camelot
- A new timeline
- A hidden Arthurian myth

---

# 3.3 Mastery

The game should reward improvement.

Players should learn:

- Enemy patterns
- Boss mechanics
- Build optimisation
- Positioning
- Timing

Failure should create knowledge.

---

# 3.4 Restoration

The player should visibly improve the world.

The first time the player returns to Camelot:

A ruined settlement.

The hundredth time:

A thriving kingdom.

The player should be able to look around and think:

"I built this."

---

# 3.5 Legacy

The final goal is not simply defeating an enemy.

The final goal is becoming worthy of the legend.

The player determines what kind of Arthur emerges.

---

# 4. Genre Positioning

## Primary Genre

Isometric Action RPG

Comparable experiences:

- Diablo IV
- Path of Exile 2
- Last Epoch
- Grim Dawn

---

## Secondary Genre

Roguelite progression.

Comparable experiences:

- Hades
- Dead Cells
- Curse of the Dead Gods

---

## Unique Identity

Unlike traditional ARPGs:

The world changes because history changes.

Unlike traditional roguelites:

The player is rebuilding a permanent kingdom.

Unlike traditional Arthurian games:

The player is not retelling the legend.

The player is creating it.

---

# 5. Core Gameplay Fantasy

The fantasy evolves throughout the game.

---

## Beginning

"I am a fallen king trying to survive."

Arthur is weak.

Camelot is gone.

The Knights are lost.

The world does not remember him.

---

## Middle

"I am restoring my kingdom."

Arthur recruits allies.

Camelot grows.

The legend returns.

The player gains influence.

---

## End

"I am the Once and Future King."

Arthur commands the restored Round Table.

Britain recognises him.

The throne awaits.

---

# 6. Game Structure

The game is built around repeated expeditions.

The basic structure:

```
Camelot Hub

↓

Choose Knight

↓

Choose Expedition

↓

Enter Fractured Britain

↓

Explore Procedural Region

↓

Fight Enemies

↓

Collect Loot

↓

Complete Events

↓

Defeat Boss

↓

Return To Camelot

↓

Restore Legend

↓

Repeat
```

---

# 7. Why Death Exists

Death is a narrative mechanic.

Arthur cannot permanently die because Guinevere anchors his soul to Avalon.

However, each resurrection has consequences.

History shifts.

The world changes.

Different possibilities emerge.

Death creates new stories.

---

# 8. The Fractured Legend Concept

Every run represents a possible history.

Examples:

## The Frozen Camelot

Arthur never returned from battle.

Camelot remains preserved beneath ice.

---

## The Saxon Victory

The Saxons conquered Britain.

Arthur must fight through the kingdom that should have been his.

---

## The Dark Avalon

The Fae corrupted the magical realm.

Nature itself attacks humanity.

---

## The Dragon Age

The old creatures returned.

Human kingdoms became prey.

---

## The Broken Round Table

The Knights turned against each other.

Arthur must reunite them.

---

# 9. Player Freedom

The game should support different interpretations of Arthur.

A player may become:

## The Warrior King

Focus:

- Strength
- Heavy weapons
- Armour
- Leadership

---

## The Holy King

Focus:

- Healing
- Light magic
- Protection
- The Grail

---

## The Arcane King

Focus:

- Merlin's teachings
- Elemental magic
- Ancient knowledge

---

## The Ruthless King

Focus:

- Damage
- Fear
- Conquest

---

# 10. Long-Term Engagement

The game should provide hundreds of hours through:

- Multiple Knights
- Procedural worlds
- Loot hunting
- Build experimentation
- Difficulty challenges
- Camelot upgrades
- Hidden lore
- Alternate endings

---

# 11. The Final Emotional Goal

The player should reach the end and feel:

"I did not simply beat a game.

I restored a legend."

Arthur: The Once and Future King should leave the player with the feeling that they participated in the creation of a myth.
