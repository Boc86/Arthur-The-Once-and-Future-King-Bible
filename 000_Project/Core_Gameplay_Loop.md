# Arthur: The Once and Future King
# Core Gameplay Loop

Version 0.1

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete gameplay cycle of Arthur: The Once and Future King.

The gameplay loop must support the central fantasy:

> Restore a broken legend, rebuild Camelot, and become worthy of the throne.

The player experience is divided into four interconnected loops:

1. Moment-to-moment combat loop
2. Expedition loop
3. Death and resurrection loop
4. Long-term legend restoration loop

---

# 2. Gameplay Overview

The complete player journey:

```
Camelot Hub

↓

Select Knight

↓

Prepare Expedition

↓

Enter Fractured Britain

Explore Procedural Zone

Fight Enemies

↓

Acquire Loot

↓

↓

Defeat Boss

↓

Return To Camelot

↓

↓

↓

Repeat
```

# 3. The Player's First Experience

## First Five Minutes

The opening must immediately establish:

- Arthur is alive again
- Britain is broken
- Guinevere is responsible for his resurrection
- Camelot is lost
- The player must rebuild the legend

---

## Opening Sequence

### Scene

A ruined battlefield.

A broken sword lies beside Arthur.

Rain falls.

The remains of knights surround him.

Arthur awakens.

---

## Guinevere

Guinevere explains:

"History has forgotten you.

But I have not."

She reveals:

- Camelot is gone
- The Knights are scattered
- The legend has fractured
- Arthur must restore what was lost

---

## First Objective

Recover a fragment of Excalibur.

The player learns:

- Movement
- Basic attack
- Dodge
- First skill
- Loot pickup
- Equipment

---

# 5. Combat Philosophy

Combat should feel:

## Responsive

Actions happen immediately.

---

## Powerful

Weapons and abilities have impact.

---

## Understandable

The player knows why they won or lost.

---

## Rewarding

Every encounter provides progress.

# 6. Expedition Loop

An expedition is the primary gameplay session.

Expected duration:

15-45 minutes.

---

```

↓

↓

Combat Encounters

↓

Events

↓

Elite Encounters

↓

Rewards

↓

Mini Boss

↓

Final Boss

↓

Return
```

---

# 7. Procedural Adventure Structure

Procedural generation does not simply create random maps.

It creates fractured histories.

Every expedition receives a Legend Seed.

Example:

```
Legend Seed:
54291

Timeline:
The Saxons conquered Britain

Region:
Dark Camelot

Modifiers:
Undead armies
Reduced healing
Increased holy damage

Special Event:
Rescue Sir Lancelot

Boss:
The Saxon Champion
```

---

# 8. Expedition Objectives

Not every run is identical.

Possible objectives:

## Recovery

Recover lost artefacts.

Example:

Find a fragment of Excalibur.

---

## Rescue

Recover important characters.

Example:

Free a corrupted Knight.

---

## Purification

Cleanse a corrupted region.

Example:

Remove Fae corruption from a forest.

---

## Hunt

Defeat legendary enemies.

Example:

Kill the Black Knight.

---

## Discovery

Explore forgotten locations.

Example:

Find Merlin's lost tower.

# 10. Death And Resurrection Loop

Death is a core gameplay mechanic.

It is not a failure state.

---

## Death Sequence

```
Arthur Falls

↓

History Fractures

↓

Guinevere Performs Ritual

↓

Arthur Awakens In Avalon

↓

Camelot Updates

↓

```

---

# 11. Why Arthur Returns

Guinevere possesses an ancient connection to Avalon.

She anchors Arthur's soul to the world.

However:

Every resurrection changes reality.

---

## Consequences Of Death

Death may alter:

- Available regions
- Enemy populations
- Events
- NPC locations
- Loot opportunities
- Timeline modifiers

- Legendary timelines
- Ancient bosses

---

# 17. Player Retention Philosophy

Players return because:

"I want better loot."

"I want to try another Knight."

"I want to see Camelot grow."

"I want to discover another version of the legend."

"I want to create my Arthur."

---

# 18. Core Loop Success Criteria

The gameplay loop succeeds when:

The player always has a reason to begin another expedition.

Every death provides progress.

Every victory changes something.

Every run tells a story.

Every restoration brings Arthur closer to becoming:

# The Once and Future King.
