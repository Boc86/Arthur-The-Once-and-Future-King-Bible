# Arthur: The Once and Future King

# SYS-033 Quest, Narrative & Lore Delivery System

Version 0.1

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how the story of Arthur: The Once and Future King is delivered.

The game combines:

- A strong authored narrative
- Procedural gameplay
- Character-driven storytelling
- Player choices
- Repeatable runs

The goal is to make every death, every battle, and every victory part of Arthur's legend.

---

# 2. Core Narrative Philosophy

The core principle:

> Arthur is not given a kingdom. He must earn one.

The player is not simply defeating enemies.

They are rebuilding Britain.

---

# 3. Narrative Pillars

The story focuses on:

```
Destiny

Leadership

Sacrifice

Loyalty

Redemption

The Cost Of Power
```

---

# 4. Story Premise

Britain has fallen.

The kingdoms have fractured.

The Round Table is broken.

The sword Excalibur has been lost.

Arthur Pendragon awakens in a ruined Camelot with only fragments of memory.

Merlin reveals:

Arthur has failed before.

Many times.

---

# 5. The Time Loop Concept

Arthur is trapped in a cycle of death and rebirth.

Every attempt:

```
Creates Experience

Restores Memories

Strengthens Camelot
```

---

# 6. Guinevere's Role

Guinevere is the reason Arthur returns.

She exists between life and death.

Each resurrection brings Arthur closer to his destiny.

---

# 7. Guinevere Narrative Function

She provides:

```
Emotional Anchor

Story Reflection

Moral Guidance

Character Growth
```

---

# 8. Merlin's Role

Merlin understands the cycle.

He knows Arthur must:

```
Gather The Knights

Restore Camelot

Defeat Morgana

Claim The Throne
```

---

# 9. Morgana's Role

Morgana is not simply evil.

She believes:

Arthur's vision of Britain is impossible.

Her philosophy:

> A kingdom built on hope will always fall.
