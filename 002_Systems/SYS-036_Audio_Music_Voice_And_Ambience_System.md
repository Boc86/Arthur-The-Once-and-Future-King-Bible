# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete audio identity of Arthur: The Once and Future King.

Audio must create:

- Emotional connection
- Combat intensity
- World immersion
- Character identity
- Legendary atmosphere

The player should feel:

> They are not playing a fantasy game. They are living inside an Arthurian legend.
